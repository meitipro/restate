# Silent demo, about ninety seconds

No voice. Every screen says what is happening, and the consensus waits are cut short with the transaction link left on screen. Record at 1280 x 800, the site in its dark theme, two browser profiles (one per wallet) both on Studio Next with test GEN.

| Time | Screen | What the viewer sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Landing, `/` | The split screen: what they said on the left, what you restated on the right, `FAIR` in the middle and "reply unlocked". It is a real thread read from the chain |
| 0:08 | Open a thread, `/new` | A topic and a position are typed, the deposit is 5 GEN, the window 24 hours. **Open the thread** is pressed and the thread page opens; its address bar is the link that is sent |
| 0:20 | Second wallet, `/t/<id>` | **Join** with the same 5 GEN, then **Restate**: the strawman "They think tokens are bad and want everyone paid in dollars." is posted |
| 0:34 | First wallet, then the verdict page, `/t/<id>/turn/1` | **Contest it**, then **Send it to the validators**. The page says "Validators are reading", the transaction link stays on screen, then the label `DISTORTED` appears with the strawman struck through. The reply box still says it is locked |
| 0:50 | Second wallet, try 2 | A full restatement is posted, contested and judged. The label `FAIR` appears and the reply box unlocks |
| 1:08 | Thread view, `/t/<id>` | The reply is posted. The rail moves to "They restate you": the author must now restate it. The money panel shows the 5 GEN deposits |

Cut the waits, never the label: each verdict screen keeps its transaction link until the label is on screen. Do not edit any text on the screen, and do not show a wallet seed or key.
