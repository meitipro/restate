---
title: Restate a position
description: Join a thread, restate the position well enough to unlock your reply, and what to do after a try fails.
---

<Callout type="warn">A restatement is not a rebuttal. Adding a claim, reversing a point, exaggerating or mocking all read as DISTORTED.</Callout>

## Join

Open a thread that has no joiner yet and press **Join**. You put in the same deposit as the author, and you must do it before the thread's window ends. You cannot join your own thread.

## Write the restatement

The restatement page puts the original beside a writing box. Say their side in your own words, so well they would sign it:

- **Keep every main point.** Leaving one out reads as `INCOMPLETE`.
- **Add nothing.** Not a counter, not a "which is silly", not your own gloss. That reads as `DISTORTED`.
- **Length is free.** A shorter restatement is FAIR if no main point is lost, and a long one is not FAIR for being long.

The page also shows a point-by-point reading aid. It is a word-overlap guess made in your browser, and it is never part of the record. The validators decide.

## After you post

The author has the window to **confirm** or **contest**. If they confirm, or the validators label it `FAIR`, your reply unlocks. If the label is `INCOMPLETE` or `DISTORTED`, the reasons are not stored, so read the original again, and try once more. A restatement gets two tries. The second failure forfeits your deposit to the author.

## Then reply

Post your reply with `rebut`. The author now has to restate it, with the same two tries. When that restatement is FAIR too, the thread is done and both deposits come back to the two balances.

## Missing a window

Every step has the thread's window. If you let it pass, anyone can call `claim_forfeit`, and your deposit goes to the other side. The one exception is a restatement waiting for the author's answer: there the window passing counts as the author contesting, `judge` sends it to the validators, and nobody forfeits.
