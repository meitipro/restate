## FAQ

**Who decides who is right?** Nobody, and Restate never tries. Validators are never told which side is which and never asked whether the original is right.

**Is it a vote?** No. Each validator reads the same two texts and answers one label. A label stands only when they agree on it.

**Can I be silent?** Not usefully. An author who does not answer inside the window counts as contesting. A joiner who does not restate, or a replier who does not reply, forfeits their deposit to the other side.

**What if I disagree with the label?** A restatement gets two tries, and the failed one stays on the page. The author can also confirm a restatement, which counts as FAIR without a validator call.

**Why can anyone call `judge` and `claim_forfeit`?** So a thread does not wait for a side that walked away. The result is a function of what is already on chain, and the caller is recorded.

**Does it ban or remove anyone?** No. There is only a locked reply box and the deposits.

**What does it cost?** The deposit, which comes back when the thread is fair, plus Studio Next's small fee deposit on every write, most of which is refunded.

**Which network?** GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997. Test GEN is free from the site's faucet.
