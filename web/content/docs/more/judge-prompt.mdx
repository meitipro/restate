## What the validators are never told

The prompt does not say which side is which, who wrote the original, or who is restating it, and it never asks whether the original is right. It asks one question, whether the restatement says what the original says, no more and no less. A restater cannot be punished for what they disagree with, only for what they got wrong about it.

## How validators agree

The leader asks its model and returns one string, the label: FAIR, INCOMPLETE or DISTORTED. Each validator asks its own model the same prompt and agrees only when its own label is exactly the leader's. Nothing else is compared and nothing else is stored from the model: no reason, no score, no quoted point. A restatement is labelled, and the label is what moves the thread.

An answer that cannot be read (not JSON, or a label outside the three) is retried once, then fails the round. It never becomes a label, because defaulting to FAIR would unlock a reply nobody earned and defaulting to a failure would spend a try on an answer nobody read.
